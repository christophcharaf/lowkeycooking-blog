I absolutely love a good stew, and this peanut butter chicken stew has become a go-to in my rotation. It’s incredibly flavorful with a comforting, creamy texture that feels so satisfying, especially on a chilly evening. Don't let the peanut butter in a savory dish fool you; it adds a wonderful depth and richness that pairs perfectly with chicken and vegetables. This recipe is genuinely quick and simple, making it totally approachable for anyone who loves delicious food but doesn't have a lot of time or experience in the kitchen.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1 tbsp soy sauce (or tamari for gluten-free)
- 1 cup frozen peas
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish (optional)
- Cooked rice or quinoa, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add chicken pieces and cook until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes. Remove chicken from the pot and set aside.
2. Add chopped onion to the pot and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in minced garlic and grated ginger, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add curry powder, cumin, and cayenne pepper (if using) to the pot. Cook, stirring, for 30 seconds to toast the spices.
5. Pour in the chicken broth, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot. Bring to a simmer.
6. In a small bowl, whisk together the peanut butter and about 1/2 cup of the hot broth until smooth.
7. Pour the peanut butter mixture into the pot along with the soy sauce. Stir well to combine.
8. Return the browned chicken to the pot. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until chicken is cooked through and tender.
9. Stir in the frozen peas and cook for another 2-3 minutes until heated through.
10. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Serve hot over cooked rice or quinoa, garnished with fresh cilantro if desired.
