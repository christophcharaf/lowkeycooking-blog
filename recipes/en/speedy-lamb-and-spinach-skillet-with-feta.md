I absolutely love finding recipes that can go from pantry to plate in under 30 minutes, and this Speedy Lamb and Spinach Skillet with Feta is a weeknight winner. It’s incredibly flavorful with the rich lamb, earthy spinach, and salty feta, yet so simple to put together. Even if you’re new to cooking, you’ll find this dish totally approachable and satisfying.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb ground lamb
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 5 oz fresh spinach
- 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add ground lamb to the skillet and cook, breaking it up with a spatula, until browned, about 5-7 minutes. Drain off any excess fat.
3. Add the chopped onion to the skillet and cook until softened, about 3-4 minutes.
4. Stir in the minced garlic, oregano, salt, and pepper. Cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
5. Add the fresh spinach to the skillet, a handful at a time, stirring until it wilts. This will take about 2-3 minutes.
6. Remove the skillet from the heat.
7. Sprinkle the crumbled feta cheese over the lamb and spinach mixture.
8. Stir gently to combine and serve immediately.
