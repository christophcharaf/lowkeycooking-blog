I love finding ways to bring big, vibrant flavors into my weeknight meals, and this Quick Skillet Jollof Rice with Chicken is a definite winner. It captures that authentic, smoky Jollof taste without all the fuss, all cooked together in one pan for minimal cleanup. Even if you're new to cooking or just looking for a reliable, impressive dish, you won't be intimidated by this recipe; it's straightforward and incredibly rewarding.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
- 1 red bell pepper, finely chopped
- 1 (15 oz) can crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup long-grain parboiled rice, rinsed
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 teaspoon curry powder
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/4 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken pieces generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet with a lid over medium-high heat. Add the chicken and cook until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes. Remove chicken from skillet and set aside.
3. Add the chopped onion to the skillet and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Stir in the minced garlic and grated ginger, cooking for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add the chopped red bell pepper and cook for another 3-4 minutes until slightly tender.
6. Pour in the crushed tomatoes, rinsed rice, chicken broth, curry powder, thyme, smoked paprika, and cayenne pepper (if using). Stir well to combine, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet.
7. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15 minutes.
8. Gently stir in the browned chicken pieces.
9. Cover again and continue to cook for another 10-15 minutes, or until the rice is tender and has absorbed most of the liquid, and the chicken is cooked through.
10. Fluff the rice with a fork, season with salt and pepper to taste.
11. Garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.
