I absolutely love a good one-pan meal, and this Quick Skillet Jollof Rice with Chicken is a weeknight savior. It captures all the vibrant, savory flavors of traditional Jollof rice without all the fuss, and the chicken cooks right alongside the rice, making cleanup a breeze. Even if you're new to cooking, you'll find this recipe incredibly forgiving and rewarding.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 1 (15 oz) can tomato sauce
- 1 cup long-grain parboiled rice, rinsed
- 2 cups chicken broth
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Season the chicken pieces generously with salt and black pepper.
2. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned chicken and cook until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes. Remove chicken from the skillet and set aside.
3. Add the chopped onion and red bell pepper to the same skillet. Sauté until softened, about 5 minutes.
4. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
5. Pour in the tomato sauce and cook for 2-3 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it darkens slightly.
6. Add the rinsed rice, curry powder, thyme, smoked paprika, and cayenne pepper (if using). Stir well to combine everything with the tomato mixture.
7. Pour in the chicken broth and bring the mixture to a boil.
8. Return the browned chicken pieces to the skillet, nestling them into the rice mixture.
9. Reduce the heat to low, cover the skillet tightly, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the rice is cooked and the liquid is absorbed.
10. Remove from heat and let stand, covered, for 5 minutes.
11. Fluff the rice gently with a fork. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley before serving.
