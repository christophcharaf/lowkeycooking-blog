I love having a go-to dessert recipe that feels special but is also incredibly simple to throw together, and this Speedy Spiced Apple Crumble is exactly that. It’s the perfect way to use up apples and fill your kitchen with the most amazing, cozy aroma. Even if you’re new to baking, you’ll find this recipe super forgiving and rewarding. It’s my favorite when I crave something warm and sweet after dinner without a lot of fuss.

## Ingredients

- 6 medium apples (about 2 lbs), peeled, cored, and sliced
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cut into cubes

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Grease a 9-inch pie plate or an 8x8 inch baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, toss the sliced apples with lemon juice, granulated sugar, 1 teaspoon cinnamon, nutmeg, and ginger. Pour the apple mixture into the prepared baking dish.
3. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the flour, brown sugar, 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon, and salt.
4. Add the cold, cubed butter to the flour mixture. Using your fingertips, a pastry blender, or two forks, cut the butter into the dry ingredients until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces of butter remaining.
5. Sprinkle the crumble topping evenly over the apples in the baking dish.
6. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the apples are tender and bubbly.
7. Let cool for at least 10 minutes before serving. Serve warm, perhaps with a scoop of vanilla ice cream or a dollop of whipped cream.
