I love finding recipes that minimize cleanup, and this one-pan harissa chicken and sweet potato hash is an absolute winner. The spicy harissa paste coats the chicken and sweet potatoes beautifully, creating a dish that's both comforting and exciting. Even if you're new to cooking, this recipe is incredibly straightforward, making it perfect for a busy weeknight when you want something delicious without the fuss.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 2 medium sweet potatoes (about 1.5 lbs), peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 large red onion, cut into wedges
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tablespoons olive oil
- 2-3 tablespoons harissa paste (adjust to your spice preference)
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro or parsley, chopped, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easier cleanup.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the cubed sweet potatoes, red onion wedges, and minced garlic.
3. Drizzle with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, then add the harissa paste, cumin, smoked paprika, salt, and pepper. Toss everything together until the vegetables are evenly coated.
4. Spread the seasoned vegetables in a single layer on one half of the prepared baking sheet.
5. Add the chicken pieces to the same mixing bowl (no need to wash it). Drizzle with the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil and season with salt and pepper. Toss to coat.
6. Arrange the seasoned chicken pieces in a single layer on the other half of the baking sheet, next to the vegetables.
7. Roast for 30-35 minutes, or until the sweet potatoes are tender and slightly caramelized, and the chicken is cooked through and lightly browned. Flip the chicken and vegetables halfway through cooking for even browning.
8. Remove from the oven. Serve the harissa chicken and sweet potato hash immediately, garnished with fresh cilantro or parsley if desired.
