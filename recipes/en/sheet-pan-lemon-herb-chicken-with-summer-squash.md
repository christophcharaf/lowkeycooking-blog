This sheet pan dinner is my go-to when I want something healthy and delicious with minimal cleanup. I love how the lemon and herbs infuse the chicken and vegetables with so much flavor, making a simple weeknight meal feel special. It’s incredibly easy to throw together, even after a long day, and perfect for beginner cooks who want impressive results without the fuss.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs or breasts, cut into 1.5-inch pieces
- 2 medium summer squash (zucchini or yellow squash), cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 red onion, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1 tbsp dried Italian seasoning
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Fresh parsley, chopped (for garnish)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easier cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, combine the chicken pieces, summer squash, and red onion.
3. Drizzle the olive oil and lemon juice over the chicken and vegetables.
4. Sprinkle the Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper over the mixture.
5. Toss everything together until the chicken and vegetables are evenly coated with the oil, lemon juice, and seasonings.
6. Spread the coated ingredients in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
7. Roast for 25-30 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are tender and slightly browned, flipping halfway through.
8. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley before serving.
