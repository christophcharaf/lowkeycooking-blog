I absolutely adore this cobbler because it tastes like summer in a pan, with all the sweet, ripe flavors of peaches and berries. It’s incredibly forgiving, even if you’re new to baking, and comes together so quickly that it’s perfect for a weeknight treat. The best part is serving it warm right out of the skillet – pure comfort!

## Ingredients

- 4 cups sliced fresh or frozen peaches (about 4-5 medium)
- 2 cups fresh or frozen blueberries
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tablespoons cornstarch
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 teaspoons baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter, cut into small cubes
- 1/2 cup milk

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
2. In a large bowl, combine the peaches, blueberries, 1/4 cup sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, vanilla extract, cinnamon, and nutmeg. Toss gently to coat the fruit.
3. Pour the fruit mixture into a 10-inch cast iron skillet or an oven-safe skillet.
4. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the flour, 1/4 cup sugar, baking powder, and salt.
5. Cut in the cold butter using a pastry blender or your fingertips until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
6. Stir in the milk until just combined, forming a thick, sticky dough.
7. Drop spoonfuls of the dough evenly over the fruit mixture in the skillet.
8. Bake for 30-35 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the fruit is bubbly.
9. Let the cobbler cool for at least 10 minutes before serving.
