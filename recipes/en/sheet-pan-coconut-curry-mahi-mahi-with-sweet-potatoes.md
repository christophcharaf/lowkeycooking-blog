I love finding recipes that make weeknight dinners feel like a treat, and this sheet pan meal is definitely one of them. The combination of the creamy coconut curry sauce, flaky mahi mahi, and sweet, tender sweet potatoes is just divine. Plus, the best part? Minimal cleanup! This recipe is so straightforward that even if you're new to cooking, you'll feel like a pro.

## Ingredients

- 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 teaspoon salt, divided
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper, divided
- 1 (13.5 ounce) can full-fat coconut milk
- 2 tablespoons red curry paste (or to taste)
- 1 tablespoon soy sauce or tamari
- 1 tablespoon lime juice
- 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
- 4 (6 ounce) mahi mahi fillets
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro, for garnish
- Lime wedges, for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easier cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, toss the sweet potato cubes with olive oil, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper. Spread them in a single layer on one half of the prepared baking sheet.
3. Roast the sweet potatoes for 15 minutes, or until they are starting to soften.
4. While the sweet potatoes are roasting, prepare the curry sauce. In a medium bowl, whisk together the coconut milk, red curry paste, soy sauce, lime juice, and grated ginger until well combined.
5. Remove the baking sheet from the oven. Push the sweet potatoes to one side and place the mahi mahi fillets on the other side.
6. Pour half of the coconut curry sauce over the mahi mahi fillets, ensuring they are evenly coated. Spoon the remaining sauce over the partially roasted sweet potatoes.
7. Season the mahi mahi with the remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper.
8. Return the baking sheet to the oven and bake for another 12-15 minutes, or until the mahi mahi is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork, and the sweet potatoes are tender.
9. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve immediately with lime wedges on the side.
