This Quick Harissa Chickpea and Vegetable Skillet is my go-to when I need a delicious and satisfying meal on the table in under 30 minutes. I love how the spicy harissa paste brings everything together, creating a depth of flavor that feels way more complex than the effort required. Even if you're new to cooking or short on time, this recipe is incredibly forgiving and yields fantastic results.

## Ingredients

- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, chopped
- 2 bell peppers (any color), chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 (15-ounce) can chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- 2-3 tablespoons harissa paste (adjust to your spice preference)
- 1 (14.5-ounce) can diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro or parsley, chopped, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the chopped onion and bell peppers to the skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 5-7 minutes.
3. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
4. Add the rinsed and drained chickpeas, harissa paste, diced tomatoes (with their juice), cumin, and smoked paprika to the skillet.
5. Stir everything together to combine, ensuring the harissa paste is well distributed.
6. Bring the mixture to a simmer, then reduce the heat to medium-low, cover, and cook for 10-12 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender and the flavors have melded.
7. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
8. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro or parsley if desired.
