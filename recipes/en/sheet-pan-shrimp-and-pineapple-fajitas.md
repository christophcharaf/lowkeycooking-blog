I love finding ways to make weeknight dinners exciting without a ton of extra work. These Sheet Pan Shrimp and Pineapple Fajitas are a game-changer! They come together so quickly, and the combination of sweet pineapple, smoky spices, and succulent shrimp is just divine. Plus, the best part is that everything cooks on one pan, meaning minimal cleanup for you. Even if you're new to cooking, you'll find this recipe totally foolproof and incredibly rewarding.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 large red bell pepper, sliced
- 1 large green bell pepper, sliced
- 1 large red onion, sliced
- 1.5 cups fresh pineapple chunks
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp onion powder
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 8 small flour or corn tortillas
- Optional toppings: lime wedges, cilantro, salsa, sour cream, avocado

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easier cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, combine the sliced bell peppers and red onion. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, then sprinkle with half of the chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and onion powder. Season with salt and pepper. Toss to coat evenly.
3. Spread the vegetables in a single layer on one half of the prepared baking sheet.
4. In the same bowl (no need to wash!), toss the shrimp and pineapple chunks with the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil and the remaining spices. Season with salt and pepper.
5. Arrange the seasoned shrimp and pineapple on the other half of the baking sheet alongside the vegetables.
6. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the shrimp are pink and cooked through, and the vegetables are tender-crisp.
7. While the fajitas are baking, warm the tortillas according to package directions.
8. Serve the shrimp and pineapple mixture immediately with the warm tortillas and your favorite toppings.
