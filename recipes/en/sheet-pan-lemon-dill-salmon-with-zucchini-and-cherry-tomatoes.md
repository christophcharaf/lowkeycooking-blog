I love finding ways to make weeknight dinners both healthy and incredibly easy, and this sheet pan salmon recipe is a winner in my book. It’s so satisfying to have a delicious meal with minimal cleanup, and this one is bursting with fresh flavors. Even if you’re new to cooking, you’ll find this recipe straightforward and forgiving.

## Ingredients

- 2 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each)
- 1 medium zucchini, chopped into 1-inch pieces
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon fresh dill, chopped
- 1 lemon, half juiced, half cut into wedges for serving
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easy cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, combine the chopped zucchini and halved cherry tomatoes. Drizzle with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, season with salt and pepper, and toss to coat.
3. Spread the zucchini and tomato mixture in a single layer on one side of the prepared baking sheet.
4. Place the salmon fillets on the other side of the baking sheet. Drizzle the salmon with the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil.
5. In a small bowl, mix together the lemon juice, chopped dill, minced garlic, salt, and pepper. Spoon this mixture evenly over the salmon fillets.
6. Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork, and the vegetables are tender.
7. Serve immediately with lemon wedges on the side.
