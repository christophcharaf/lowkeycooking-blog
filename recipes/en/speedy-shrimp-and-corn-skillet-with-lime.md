I absolutely love this Speedy Shrimp and Corn Skillet when I'm craving something delicious but short on time. It comes together so fast, making it perfect for a weeknight dinner that feels special without any fuss. The combination of sweet corn, succulent shrimp, and a bright lime kick is incredibly satisfying, and the best part is that it's all made in one pan, meaning less cleanup for me! Even if you're new to cooking, you'll find this recipe incredibly straightforward and rewarding.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 teaspoon chili powder
- 1/2 teaspoon cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 2 cups corn kernels (fresh or frozen)
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
- 1 lime, juiced

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add shrimp to the skillet. Sprinkle with chili powder, cumin, salt, and pepper. Cook for 1-2 minutes per side, until pink and opaque. Remove shrimp from the skillet and set aside.
3. Add corn kernels and sliced red onion to the same skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, for about 5-7 minutes, until the corn is tender and slightly charred, and the onion has softened.
4. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for another minute until fragrant.
5. Return the cooked shrimp to the skillet. Add the lime juice and chopped cilantro.
6. Toss everything together to combine and heat through for about 1-2 minutes.
7. Serve immediately.
