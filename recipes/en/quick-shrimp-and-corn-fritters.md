I absolutely love how quick and easy these shrimp and corn fritters are to whip up. They remind me of summer afternoons, and they’re so versatile – perfect as a light lunch, an appetizer, or even a side dish. Don't be intimidated by frying; these come together in a flash and are incredibly forgiving for beginner cooks. The combination of sweet corn and succulent shrimp is just delightful, and they get wonderfully crispy on the outside.

## Ingredients

- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 large egg, lightly beaten
- 1/2 cup milk
- 1/4 cup chopped fresh chives
- 1 cup corn kernels (fresh or frozen, thawed)
- 1/2 cup cooked small shrimp, chopped
- Vegetable oil, for frying

## Instructions

1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt, and pepper.
2. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the egg, milk, and chives.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until just combined. Do not overmix.
4. Gently fold in the corn kernels and chopped shrimp.
5. Heat about 1/2 inch of vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
6. Drop spoonfuls of the batter into the hot oil, being careful not to overcrowd the skillet.
7. Fry for 2-3 minutes per side, until golden brown and cooked through.
8. Remove fritters with a slotted spoon and drain on paper towels.
9. Serve immediately.
