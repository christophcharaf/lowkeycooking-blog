I love meals that are as easy to clean up as they are to make, and this sheet pan salmon is definitely one of them. It’s my go-to when I’m craving something healthy and delicious but don’t have a lot of time or energy. The lemon and herbs infuse the salmon beautifully, and the roasted vegetables are the perfect accompaniment. Even if you're new to cooking, you'll find this recipe totally foolproof.

## Ingredients

- 4 (6-ounce) salmon fillets, skin on or off
- 1 pound asparagus, trimmed
- 1 pint cherry tomatoes
- 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 lemon, thinly sliced, plus more for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easy cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, toss the trimmed asparagus and cherry tomatoes with 2 tablespoons of olive oil, minced garlic, oregano, thyme, salt, and pepper. Spread the vegetables in a single layer on one side of the prepared baking sheet.
3. Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Drizzle the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil over the salmon and season with salt and pepper.
4. Place the seasoned salmon fillets on the other side of the baking sheet, next to the vegetables. Top each salmon fillet with a few thin slices of lemon.
5. Bake for 15-20 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork, and the asparagus is tender-crisp. Cooking time will vary depending on the thickness of your salmon.
6. Serve the salmon and vegetables immediately, with extra lemon wedges on the side.
