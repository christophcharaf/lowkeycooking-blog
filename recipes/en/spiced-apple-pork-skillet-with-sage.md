This Spiced Apple Pork Skillet with Sage is one of my go-to weeknight meals when I'm craving something hearty and flavorful without a lot of fuss. The combination of savory pork and sweet, spiced apples is just divine, and the fresh sage really elevates the whole dish. It's incredibly easy to make, all in one pan, which means less cleanup for me, and I promise it's simple enough for even beginner cooks to nail.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless pork loin, cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp ground cloves
- 1 tsp salt, divided
- 1/2 tsp black pepper, divided
- 2 medium apples (like Honeycrisp or Fuji), cored and sliced
- 1 medium yellow onion, thinly sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup chicken broth
- 2 tbsp fresh sage leaves, chopped

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, toss the pork cubes with olive oil, cinnamon, nutmeg, cloves, 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper.
2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned pork in a single layer and sear until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes. Remove pork from skillet and set aside.
3. Add the sliced apples and onion to the same skillet. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the onion is softened and the apples are beginning to caramelize, about 6-8 minutes.
4. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
5. Pour in the chicken broth and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet.
6. Return the seared pork to the skillet. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to medium-low, cover, and cook for 10-12 minutes, or until the pork is cooked through and tender.
7. Stir in the chopped fresh sage, the remaining 1/2 teaspoon salt, and 1/4 teaspoon black pepper.
8. Serve hot, directly from the skillet.
