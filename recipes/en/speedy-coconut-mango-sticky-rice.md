I absolutely adore Thai mango sticky rice, but sometimes I just don't have the patience for the traditional method. This speedy version captures all the delicious flavors and textures without the long soaking and steaming times. It's perfect for when a craving strikes and you want a sweet, comforting treat in a flash. Don't be intimidated by the "sticky rice" – it's much simpler than it sounds!

## Ingredients

- 1.5 cups glutinous rice (sweet rice)
- 1 can (13.5 oz) full-fat coconut milk, divided
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 2 ripe mangoes, peeled and sliced
- Toasted sesame seeds, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Rinse the glutinous rice under cold water until the water runs clear. Cook the rice according to your rice cooker's instructions for white rice. If you don't have a rice cooker, cook it in a pot on the stovetop according to package directions, or until tender and slightly chewy.
2. While the rice is cooking, prepare the coconut sauce. In a small saucepan, combine 1 cup of coconut milk, granulated sugar, and salt. Heat over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, until the sugar and salt are dissolved and the mixture is smooth. Do not boil. Remove from heat.
3. Once the rice is cooked, transfer it to a medium bowl. Pour about 3/4 of the warm coconut sauce over the hot rice. Gently stir to combine, ensuring all the rice is coated. Cover the bowl and let it sit for 10-15 minutes to allow the rice to absorb the sauce.
4. While the rice is resting, gently warm the remaining 1/4 cup of coconut milk in the small saucepan (or microwave). This will be used for drizzling.
5. To serve, spoon the sticky rice into bowls. Arrange the sliced mangoes alongside the rice. Drizzle the remaining warm coconut sauce over the rice and mangoes. Garnish with toasted sesame seeds, if desired. Serve immediately.
