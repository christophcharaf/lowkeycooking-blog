I absolutely love a good hash, and this Spiced Lamb and Sweet Potato Hash is one of my favorites. It's incredibly satisfying, packed with flavor from the lamb and warming spices, and the sweetness of the potatoes perfectly balances everything out. I often make a big batch on the weekend to have for easy lunches during the week. Don't be intimidated by the lamb; it cooks up beautifully in the skillet, and this recipe is straightforward enough for any home cook.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground lamb
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and diced (about 3 cups)
- 1 large red onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro or parsley, chopped, for garnish (optional)
- 4 large eggs (optional, for serving)

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced sweet potatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, until they begin to soften and brown, about 10-12 minutes.
2. Add the chopped red onion to the skillet and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Push the sweet potatoes and onions to one side of the skillet. Add the ground lamb to the empty side and cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned.
4. Stir in the minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, cinnamon, and cayenne pepper (if using). Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Combine the lamb mixture with the sweet potatoes and onions. Season generously with salt and black pepper.
6. Reduce the heat to medium-low, cover the skillet, and let it cook for another 10-15 minutes, or until the sweet potatoes are tender and the flavors have melded. Stir occasionally.
7. If serving with eggs, create four wells in the hash and crack an egg into each well. Cover the skillet and cook until the egg whites are set and yolks are still runny, about 3-5 minutes.
8. Garnish with fresh cilantro or parsley, if desired, and serve hot.
