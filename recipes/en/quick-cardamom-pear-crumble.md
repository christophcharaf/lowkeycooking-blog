I love a good crumble, and this Quick Cardamom Pear Crumble is one of my absolute favorites for a weeknight treat. It's incredibly simple to throw together, even if you're new to baking, and the aroma of warm pears and cardamom filling your kitchen is just divine. The best part is that it uses common pantry staples, making it a go-to dessert whenever a craving strikes. You'll be amazed at how quickly you can go from pantry to plate with this comforting dish.

## Ingredients

- 4 ripe pears (like Bosc or Anjou), peeled, cored, and sliced
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice
- 1 teaspoon ground cardamom
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup packed brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) cold unsalted butter, cut into cubes

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease an 8x8 inch baking dish.
2. In a medium bowl, toss the sliced pears with lemon juice, ground cardamom, and granulated sugar. Spread the pear mixture evenly in the prepared baking dish.
3. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt.
4. Add the cold, cubed butter to the dry ingredients. Use your fingertips or a pastry blender to cut the butter into the flour mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.
5. Sprinkle the crumble topping evenly over the pear mixture in the baking dish.
6. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the pear filling is bubbly.
7. Let cool for at least 10 minutes before serving. Serve warm, perhaps with a scoop of vanilla ice cream or a dollop of whipped cream.
