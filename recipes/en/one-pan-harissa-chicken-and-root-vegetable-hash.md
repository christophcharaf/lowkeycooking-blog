I love a good sheet pan dinner, and this One-Pan Harissa Chicken and Root Vegetable Hash is a weeknight lifesaver. The smoky, spicy harissa paste coats the chicken and vegetables beautifully, creating a symphony of flavors and textures. It’s incredibly satisfying and requires minimal cleanup, which is always a win in my book. Even if you're new to cooking, you'll find this recipe straightforward and forgiving.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 lb sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch cubes
- 1 lb carrots, peeled and cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 large red onion, cut into 1-inch wedges
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2-3 tbsp harissa paste (adjust to your spice preference)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro or parsley, chopped, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easier cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, combine the cubed chicken thighs, sweet potatoes, carrots, and red onion wedges.
3. Add the minced garlic, olive oil, harissa paste, cumin, and smoked paprika to the bowl. Season generously with salt and pepper.
4. Toss everything together until the chicken and vegetables are evenly coated with the harissa mixture.
5. Spread the mixture in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet. Ensure the ingredients are not overcrowded, as this will steam them rather than roast them.
6. Roast for 35-40 minutes, or until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are tender and slightly caramelized. Flip the ingredients halfway through cooking for even browning.
7. Remove from the oven and garnish with fresh cilantro or parsley, if desired. Serve hot.
