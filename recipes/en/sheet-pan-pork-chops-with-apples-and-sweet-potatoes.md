I love finding meals that are as easy to clean up as they are to make, and this sheet pan dinner is one of my absolute favorites. The combination of savory pork chops, sweet apples, and earthy sweet potatoes creates a perfectly balanced and comforting meal. You don't need to be an expert chef to pull this off; it's incredibly straightforward, making it ideal for busy weeknights or when you're just starting out in the kitchen.

## Ingredients

- 4 (6-ounce) bone-in pork chops
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed (about 1-inch pieces)
- 2 medium apples (like Honeycrisp or Fuji), cored and cut into 1-inch wedges
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easier cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, toss the cubed sweet potatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, pepper, and half of the smoked paprika, garlic powder, and thyme. Spread them in a single layer on one side of the prepared baking sheet.
3. Roast the sweet potatoes for 15 minutes.
4. While the sweet potatoes roast, pat the pork chops dry with paper towels. Rub them with the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, pepper, and the remaining spices.
5. Add the apple wedges to the same bowl you used for the sweet potatoes (no need to wash it). Toss them with a pinch of salt and pepper.
6. After 15 minutes, remove the baking sheet from the oven. Add the seasoned pork chops to the empty side of the baking sheet, nestled among the sweet potatoes. Scatter the seasoned apple wedges around the pork chops and sweet potatoes.
7. Return the baking sheet to the oven and roast for another 20-25 minutes, or until the pork chops are cooked through (internal temperature of 145°F or 63°C) and the sweet potatoes are tender.
8. Let the pork chops rest for a few minutes before serving alongside the roasted apples and sweet potatoes.
