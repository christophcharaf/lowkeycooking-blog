I absolutely adore this Spiced Pear and Walnut Crumble, especially on a chilly evening. The aroma of warm pears and cinnamon filling my kitchen is pure comfort. It's a classic dessert that feels fancy but is surprisingly simple to make. Even if you're new to baking, you'll find this recipe incredibly forgiving and rewarding.

## Ingredients

**For the Filling:**
- 6 medium ripe pears (like Bosc or Anjou), peeled, cored, and sliced
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice

**For the Crumble Topping:**
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) cold unsalted butter, cut into cubes

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease an 8x8 inch baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, combine the sliced pears, 2 tablespoons flour, granulated sugar, 1 teaspoon cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice. Toss gently to coat the pears evenly.
3. Pour the pear mixture into the prepared baking dish and spread it out evenly.
4. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together 1 cup flour, brown sugar, chopped walnuts, 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon, and salt.
5. Add the cold, cubed butter to the dry ingredients. Use a pastry blender, your fingertips, or two forks to cut the butter into the flour mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs.
6. Sprinkle the crumble topping evenly over the pear filling.
7. Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the pear filling is bubbly.
8. Let the crumble cool slightly before serving. It's delicious served warm on its own or with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
