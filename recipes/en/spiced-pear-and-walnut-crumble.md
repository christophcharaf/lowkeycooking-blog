This Spiced Pear and Walnut Crumble is my go-to dessert when I'm craving something sweet, cozy, and incredibly easy to make. The aroma that fills my kitchen as it bakes is simply heavenly, and the combination of tender, spiced pears with the crunchy, buttery topping is pure comfort. Even if you're new to baking, you'll find this recipe straightforward and forgiving, promising a delicious treat without any fuss.

## Ingredients

**For the Filling:**
- 6 medium ripe pears (like Bosc or Anjou), peeled, cored, and sliced
- 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
- 1 tablespoon lemon juice

**For the Crumble Topping:**
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
- 1/2 cup rolled oats (not instant)
- 1/2 cup chopped walnuts
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) cold unsalted butter, cut into small cubes

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a 9-inch pie plate or an 8x8 inch baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, combine the sliced pears, 2 tablespoons of flour, granulated sugar, 1 teaspoon cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice. Toss gently to coat the pears evenly.
3. Pour the pear mixture into the prepared baking dish and spread it out evenly.
4. In a separate medium bowl, whisk together the 1 cup flour, brown sugar, rolled oats, chopped walnuts, 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon, and salt for the crumble topping.
5. Add the cold, cubed butter to the dry ingredients. Use your fingertips, a pastry blender, or a fork to cut the butter into the mixture until it resembles coarse crumbs with some pea-sized pieces of butter remaining.
6. Sprinkle the crumble topping evenly over the pear filling in the baking dish.
7. Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until the topping is golden brown and the filling is bubbly.
8. Let the crumble cool for at least 10-15 minutes before serving. It's delicious served warm on its own or with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
