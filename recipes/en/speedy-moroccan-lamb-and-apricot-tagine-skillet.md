I absolutely adore how this Speedy Moroccan Lamb and Apricot Tagine Skillet brings a taste of exotic flavors right into my kitchen with minimal fuss. It's one of those dishes that feels incredibly special and complex, yet it comes together surprisingly fast, making it perfect for a weeknight meal. Even if you're new to cooking or a bit intimidated by "exotic" spices, this recipe is designed to be super accessible and forgiving, resulting in a wonderfully aromatic and satisfying dinner.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 lb ground lamb
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 (14.5 oz) can diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 cup chicken or vegetable broth
- 1/2 cup dried apricots, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish
- Cooked couscous or rice, for serving

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the ground lamb and cook, breaking it up with a spoon, until browned. Drain off any excess fat.
2. Add the chopped onion to the skillet and cook until softened, about 5 minutes. Stir in the minced garlic, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, cayenne pepper (if using), salt, and black pepper. Cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
3. Pour in the diced tomatoes (with their juice) and the broth. Bring the mixture to a simmer, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet.
4. Stir in the chopped dried apricots. Reduce the heat to low, cover, and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the sauce has thickened slightly and the flavors have melded.
5. Taste and adjust seasoning if necessary.
6. Serve the tagine hot over cooked couscous or rice, garnished with fresh cilantro.
