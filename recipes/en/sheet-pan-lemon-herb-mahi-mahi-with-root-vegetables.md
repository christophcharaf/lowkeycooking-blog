I love a good sheet pan dinner, and this one is a personal favorite for weeknights. It's so satisfying to get a complete, flavorful meal on the table with minimal cleanup. The lemon and herbs really brighten up the rich, earthy root vegetables and the delicate mahi mahi. You don't need to be an experienced cook to nail this recipe; it's straightforward and forgiving, perfect for anyone wanting a delicious, healthy meal without the fuss.

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs mixed root vegetables (such as carrots, parsnips, sweet potatoes), peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
- 2 tbsp olive oil, divided
- 1 tsp dried rosemary
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 lb mahi mahi fillets, patted dry
- 1 lemon, half thinly sliced, half juiced
- 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easier cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, toss the chopped root vegetables with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, rosemary, thyme, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
3. Spread the seasoned vegetables in a single layer on one half of the prepared baking sheet.
4. Roast the vegetables for 15 minutes.
5. While the vegetables are roasting, pat the mahi mahi fillets dry with paper towels. Drizzle the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil over the fish and season with salt and pepper.
6. After 15 minutes, remove the baking sheet from the oven. Add the mahi mahi fillets to the empty half of the baking sheet. Place the lemon slices on top of the fish.
7. Return the baking sheet to the oven and roast for another 12-15 minutes, or until the fish is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork, and the vegetables are tender.
8. Squeeze the juice from the remaining lemon half over the fish and vegetables. Sprinkle with fresh parsley.
9. Serve immediately.
