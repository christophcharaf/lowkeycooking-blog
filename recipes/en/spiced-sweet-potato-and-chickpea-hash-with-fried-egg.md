This spiced sweet potato and chickpea hash is one of my go-to weekend breakfasts when I want something satisfying and packed with flavor. It feels so comforting and wholesome, and the combination of the sweet potatoes, earthy chickpeas, and warm spices is just divine. Plus, topping it all off with a perfectly fried egg makes it a complete meal that’s surprisingly easy to whip up, even for beginner cooks.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 medium sweet potato, peeled and diced into ½-inch cubes
- ½ red onion, diced
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
- ¼ teaspoon turmeric
- Pinch of cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 (15-ounce) can chickpeas, rinsed and drained
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 2 large eggs
- Fresh cilantro or parsley, chopped, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the diced sweet potato and red onion. Cook, stirring occasionally, for about 10-12 minutes, or until the sweet potato is tender and slightly browned.
2. Stir in the minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, turmeric, and cayenne pepper (if using). Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add the rinsed and drained chickpeas to the skillet. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Stir everything together and cook for another 5-7 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld and the chickpeas to heat through.
4. While the hash is finishing, heat a separate non-stick skillet or lightly oil the same skillet (if serving immediately and you don't mind a little overlap) over medium heat. Crack the eggs into the skillet and fry them to your desired doneness (sunny-side up or over-easy are great here).
5. Divide the sweet potato and chickpea hash between two plates.
6. Top each serving of hash with a fried egg. Garnish with fresh cilantro or parsley, if desired. Serve immediately.
