I love a good hash because it feels so rustic and comforting, and this spiced lamb and sweet potato version is a weeknight winner. It comes together surprisingly fast, and the combination of savory lamb, sweet potatoes, and aromatic spices is just divine. Don't be intimidated by the spices; I’ve picked ones that are warm and approachable, making this an easy dish even for beginner cooks.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb ground lamb
- 2 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and diced into 1/2-inch cubes
- 1 medium red onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper (optional, for heat)
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish (optional)
- 4 large eggs (optional, for serving)

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the diced sweet potatoes and cook, stirring occasionally, for about 8-10 minutes until they begin to soften and brown.
2. Add the chopped red onion to the skillet and cook for another 5 minutes until softened.
3. Push the sweet potatoes and onions to one side of the skillet. Add the ground lamb to the other side. Break up the lamb with a spoon and cook until browned, about 5-7 minutes.
4. Stir in the minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, cinnamon, and cayenne pepper (if using). Cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Combine the lamb mixture with the sweet potatoes and onions. Season generously with salt and pepper. Cook for another 2-3 minutes, stirring to combine all the flavors.
6. If desired, make four wells in the hash and crack an egg into each. Cover the skillet and cook for 3-5 minutes, or until the egg whites are set but the yolks are still runny.
7. Garnish with fresh parsley, if desired, and serve immediately.
