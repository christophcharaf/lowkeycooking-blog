This recipe for Lemon-Dill Salmon with Asparagus and Potatoes is my go-to when I want a healthy and delicious meal on the table without a lot of fuss. I love how the bright, fresh flavors of lemon and dill complement the rich salmon and tender vegetables. It feels like a fancy restaurant meal, but it's surprisingly simple to make, even if you're new to cooking. You'll be amazed at how easily everything comes together on one pan!

## Ingredients

- 1.5 lbs small potatoes (like Yukon Gold or red potatoes), quartered
- 1 lb asparagus, trimmed
- 4 (6-ounce) salmon fillets, skin on or off
- 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
- 1 teaspoon dried dill (or 1 tablespoon fresh, chopped)
- 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 lemon, half juiced, half cut into wedges for serving

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easy cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, toss the quartered potatoes with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Spread them in a single layer on one half of the prepared baking sheet.
3. Roast the potatoes for 15 minutes.
4. While the potatoes roast, toss the trimmed asparagus with 1 tablespoon of olive oil, salt, and pepper in the same bowl.
5. After 15 minutes, add the asparagus to the other half of the baking sheet alongside the potatoes.
6. Pat the salmon fillets dry with paper towels. Drizzle the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil over the salmon, then season with dill, salt, and pepper.
7. Place the seasoned salmon fillets on top of the asparagus.
8. Return the baking sheet to the oven and roast for another 12-15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork, and the asparagus is tender-crisp.
9. Squeeze fresh lemon juice over the salmon and vegetables just before serving. Serve immediately with lemon wedges on the side.
