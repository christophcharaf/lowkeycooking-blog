This Spiced Pear and Oatmeal Breakfast Bake is my go-to for a cozy weekend morning or even a make-ahead weekday treat. The combination of tender, spiced pears with hearty oats is just so satisfying, and the aroma that fills the kitchen while it bakes is pure comfort. Don't be intimidated by baking; this recipe is incredibly straightforward and requires minimal effort for a truly delicious result.

## Ingredients

- 2 cups rolled oats (not instant)
- 1/4 cup brown sugar, packed
- 1 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1/2 teaspoon nutmeg
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 large pears, peeled, cored, and diced
- 1/2 cup milk (dairy or non-dairy)
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Lightly grease a 8x8 inch baking dish.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the rolled oats, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and salt. Stir well to distribute the spices evenly.
3. Add the diced pears to the dry ingredients and toss gently to coat them.
4. In a separate, smaller bowl, whisk together the milk, melted butter, egg, and vanilla extract until well combined.
5. Pour the wet ingredients over the oat and pear mixture. Stir everything together until just combined, ensuring all the oats are moistened.
6. Pour the mixture into the prepared baking dish and spread it out evenly.
7. Bake for 35-40 minutes, or until the top is golden brown and the edges are set.
8. Let it cool for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy warm, perhaps with a dollop of yogurt or a drizzle of maple syrup.
