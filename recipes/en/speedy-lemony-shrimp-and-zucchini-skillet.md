I love finding recipes that are not only delicious but also incredibly quick to put together. This Speedy Lemony Shrimp and Zucchini Skillet is one of those gems that has become a go-to for me when I'm short on time but still craving something fresh and satisfying. It’s packed with bright, vibrant flavors and comes together so fast, you'll hardly believe it’s ready in under 30 minutes. Don't be intimidated by the fresh ingredients; this dish is super forgiving and perfect for even beginner cooks.

## Ingredients

- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 pound large shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 medium zucchinis, trimmed and cut into half-moons
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/4 teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional)
- 1/4 cup dry white wine or chicken broth
- 2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
2. Add the shrimp to the skillet in a single layer and cook for 1-2 minutes per side, until pink and slightly opaque. Remove the shrimp from the skillet and set aside.
3. Add the zucchini to the same skillet and cook, stirring occasionally, for 4-5 minutes, until tender-crisp.
4. Stir in the minced garlic and red pepper flakes (if using) and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
5. Pour in the white wine or chicken broth and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the skillet. Let it simmer for 1 minute.
6. Return the cooked shrimp to the skillet. Stir in the lemon juice and chopped parsley.
7. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Cook for another 1-2 minutes, until the shrimp are heated through and the sauce has slightly thickened.
8. Serve immediately.
