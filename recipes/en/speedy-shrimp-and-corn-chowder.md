This Speedy Shrimp and Corn Chowder is my go-to for a quick, comforting weeknight meal. I love how it feels so hearty and satisfying, yet comes together in a flash. Even if you're new to cooking or short on time, this recipe is incredibly forgiving and guarantees a delicious outcome. The blend of sweet corn and tender shrimp in a creamy broth is simply divine.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 cup chopped yellow onion
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups chicken broth
- 2 cups frozen corn kernels
- 1 lb shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 1 cup milk (or half-and-half for richer chowder)
- 2 tbsp chopped fresh parsley, for garnish (optional)
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat olive oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium heat. Add the chopped onion and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
3. Pour in the chicken broth and add the frozen corn. Bring the mixture to a simmer.
4. Add the shrimp to the simmering broth. Cook for 3-5 minutes, or until the shrimp turn pink and opaque. Be careful not to overcook.
5. Stir in the milk (or half-and-half) and heat gently until warmed through. Do not boil after adding the milk.
6. Season with salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste.
7. Ladle the chowder into bowls and garnish with fresh parsley, if desired. Serve immediately.
