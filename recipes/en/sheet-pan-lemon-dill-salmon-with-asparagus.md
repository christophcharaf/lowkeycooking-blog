This sheet pan salmon with asparagus is my go-to for those nights when I want something healthy and delicious with minimal cleanup. I love how the bright lemon and fresh dill complement the rich salmon, and everything cooks perfectly in one oven. Even if you're new to cooking, this recipe is incredibly straightforward and results in a restaurant-worthy meal in under 30 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 2 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each)
- 1 lb asparagus, trimmed
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon fresh dill, chopped
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- 1 lemon, thinly sliced (optional, for serving)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easy cleanup.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, minced garlic, chopped dill, salt, and pepper.
3. Place the trimmed asparagus on one side of the prepared baking sheet and drizzle with about half of the lemon-dill mixture. Toss to coat evenly.
4. Place the salmon fillets on the other side of the baking sheet. Spoon the remaining lemon-dill mixture over the top of each salmon fillet.
5. Arrange the lemon slices on top of the salmon, if using.
6. Bake for 12-15 minutes, or until the salmon is cooked through and flakes easily with a fork, and the asparagus is tender-crisp.
7. Serve immediately, with extra lemon wedges if desired.
