I love starting my day with something that feels a little special, and these Lemon Blueberry Yogurt Parfaits are just that! They're bursting with fresh, vibrant flavors and are incredibly satisfying without feeling heavy. The best part? They take literally minutes to assemble, making them perfect for busy mornings or when you need a quick, healthy treat. Even if you're new to cooking, you can totally nail this one!

## Ingredients

- 2 cups plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice
- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup, plus more for drizzling
- 1 teaspoon lemon zest
- 1.5 cups fresh blueberries
- 1 cup granola

## Instructions

1. In a small bowl, combine the Greek yogurt, lemon juice, honey (or maple syrup), and lemon zest. Stir until well combined and smooth.
2. Spoon about 2 tablespoons of the yogurt mixture into the bottom of each serving glass.
3. Top the yogurt layer with about 1/4 cup of blueberries.
4. Sprinkle about 2 tablespoons of granola over the blueberries.
5. Repeat the layers: another layer of yogurt, followed by blueberries, and then granola.
6. Drizzle with a little extra honey or maple syrup, if desired. Serve immediately or chill for later.
