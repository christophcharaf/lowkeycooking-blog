I love having a quick and easy meal option that also feels incredibly satisfying, and these black bean and corn quesadillas are exactly that. They’re perfect for those busy weeknights when you want something delicious without a lot of fuss. You can even customize them with your favorite salsa or a dollop of sour cream. Don't be intimidated by the cooking process; these are incredibly beginner-friendly!

## Ingredients

- 8 (8-inch) flour tortillas
- 1 (15-ounce) can black beans, rinsed and drained
- 1 cup frozen or canned corn, drained
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1/2 cup chopped fresh cilantro (optional)
- 1/4 teaspoon chili powder
- 1/4 teaspoon cumin
- 1 tablespoon olive oil or butter

## Instructions

1. In a medium bowl, combine the rinsed black beans, corn, shredded cheese, cilantro (if using), chili powder, and cumin. Mix well.
2. Lay out four tortillas. Spoon about 1/4 of the bean and corn mixture onto one half of each tortilla.
3. Fold the other half of the tortilla over the filling to create a half-moon shape.
4. Heat the olive oil or butter in a large skillet over medium heat.
5. Carefully place two quesadillas into the hot skillet. Cook for 3-4 minutes per side, until golden brown and the cheese is melted.
6. Remove the cooked quesadillas from the skillet and set aside. Repeat with the remaining two quesadillas.
7. Cut each quesadilla into wedges and serve immediately.
