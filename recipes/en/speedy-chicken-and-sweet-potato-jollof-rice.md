I absolutely love a good one-pot meal, and this Speedy Chicken and Sweet Potato Jollof Rice is a weeknight lifesaver. It’s packed with incredible flavor from the spices and the sweetness of the potatoes, making it feel like a special occasion meal, but it comes together so easily. Even if you’re new to cooking, you’ll find this recipe straightforward and incredibly rewarding.

## Ingredients

- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1-inch piece fresh ginger, grated
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp dried thyme
- ¼ tsp cayenne pepper (optional)
- 1 (15 oz) can diced tomatoes, undrained
- 1 cup chicken broth
- 1 large sweet potato (about 1 lb), peeled and diced into ½-inch cubes
- 1 ½ cups long-grain white rice, rinsed
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro, chopped, for garnish

## Instructions

1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the chicken pieces and cook until browned on all sides, about 5-7 minutes. Remove chicken from the pot and set aside.
2. Add the chopped onion to the pot and cook until softened, about 5 minutes.
3. Stir in the minced garlic and grated ginger, and cook for 1 minute more until fragrant.
4. Add the curry powder, smoked paprika, thyme, and cayenne pepper (if using). Stir well and cook for 30 seconds to toast the spices.
5. Pour in the diced tomatoes and chicken broth. Bring to a simmer, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom of the pot.
6. Stir in the diced sweet potato and rinsed rice. Return the browned chicken to the pot.
7. Season with salt and pepper. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat to low, cover the pot tightly, and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the rice is cooked and the liquid is absorbed.
8. Let the jollof rice stand, covered, for 5 minutes off the heat.
9. Fluff the rice with a fork, gently incorporating the chicken and sweet potato.
10. Serve hot, garnished with fresh cilantro.
