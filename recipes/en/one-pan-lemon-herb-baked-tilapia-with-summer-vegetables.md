I love finding recipes that are not only delicious but also incredibly simple to clean up, and this one-pan tilapia dish is a winner. It’s the perfect weeknight meal when I’m craving something fresh and healthy without spending hours in the kitchen. The combination of flaky fish, tender vegetables, and zesty lemon and herbs is just divine. Even if you're new to cooking, this recipe is totally foolproof; just chop, toss, and bake!

## Ingredients

- 4 (6-ounce) tilapia fillets
- 1 pound small zucchini, cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 pound cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 red onion, cut into 1-inch chunks
- 1 lemon, thinly sliced, plus more for serving
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 tablespoon dried Italian seasoning
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste
- Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Line a large baking sheet with parchment paper for easy cleanup.
2. In a large bowl, combine the zucchini, cherry tomatoes, and red onion. Drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with Italian seasoning, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Toss to coat the vegetables evenly.
3. Spread the seasoned vegetables in a single layer on the prepared baking sheet.
4. Place the tilapia fillets on top of the vegetables. Season the fish with a little more salt and pepper.
5. Arrange the lemon slices over and around the tilapia fillets.
6. Bake for 18-25 minutes, or until the tilapia is opaque and flakes easily with a fork, and the vegetables are tender and slightly caramelized. Cooking time will vary depending on the thickness of the fish.
7. Garnish with fresh parsley, if desired. Serve immediately with extra lemon wedges.
